# UI

The rebuild is the chance to improve every screen, and this is the record of it. Behaviour, rules and copy come from v0 and `SPEC.md`; the look does not. Before a card builds a surface, a standalone mock of it lands under `docs/mocks/` as one HTML file that opens on its own (real tokens, real type, the light and dark themes, a phone width), the owner approves it, and the row below is filled in. A surface without an approved mock is not built.

What every mock has to keep: the ten-second rule (home is the tool, near-zero words, one primary button, everything else behind the account menu), the tokens in `frontend/app/globals.css` (the accent colours what you read, the button is ink), lucide glyphs, and type sized for a phone filming a laptop.

| Proof the round is being heard | (new) | `mocks/recording.html` | The microphone's health is said during the round instead of after it. Today a microphone that opened and heard nothing is only reported on the done screen, sixty seconds too late, and one that never opened at all draws nothing at all, on that round and on every round after it: for the whole minute a working round and a broken one look identical, so there is no moment at which anybody could have fixed it. Three states, three places. **Heard** is a level meter under the clock, five bars on the centre line the report's wave is already drawn on, moving with the voice, with no label and no icon, because a meter that is moving is already the sentence. **Open but silent** (muted hardware, the wrong input, a headset that never connected) is caught on the topic screen before the clock, since the level ticker is already running there and a live microphone in a silent room is never digital silence: exact zero for two seconds is a dead input and not a quiet room. **Heard nothing once you began** is caught four seconds into the speaking, when the bars have not moved: they go warm and flat and one sentence says so, and the clock never stops, because a broken microphone is not a broken round. Nothing new is measured; the level is already sampled fifty times a second for the timeline and this only draws it. The two indicators that were drawn and not taken are kept in the mock's fourth tab: the report's own wave drawn live, which would show somebody their pauses while they are still in them, and a breathing pulse, which says something is on but not that it is hearing you. | approved 2026-09-08 and built the same day on card 37; the meter was the owner's call over the wave and the pulse, and its scale changed during the build (see DECISIONS): full height is a shout and the climb to it is a square root, because a linear meter read dead for exactly the person talking normally |
