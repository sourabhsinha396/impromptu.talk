The challenge link: proposal, not yet decided

Not v0 parity - v0 had no such thing - so it needs its own case, logged in docs/DECISIONS.md once the owner picks a shape. Nothing here is built. It is the cheapest of the growth ideas because most of it already exists: /?topic=<slug> already hands somebody one exact prompt and skips the spinner (SPEC.md §3).

What it is

  • A dare you send to one person. You finish a round, you think a friend would struggle with that topic, you send them a link. They open it and the topic is already on screen. That is the whole feature.
  • The link carries a word they have to say. This is what makes it a dare rather than a link. It is optional, one word, and it is checked: the transcript and word timings are already stored for every round, so "you said it at 0:34" is arithmetic, not a model call and not a new measurement. A dare with no word is still a dare, just a quieter one.
  • The thread is the feed. Nothing comes back to the sender automatically, no result is published, nobody is ranked. The receiver's done screen offers "Dare them back", which makes a new link they paste into the same chat. WhatsApp does the social half and we build none of it, which keeps the never list intact (SPEC.md §14: no leaderboards, no social feed).
  • Free, both ends. Making and receiving are free forever. This is funnel, not product: PRICING.md §2 already refuses to paywall the streak for the same reason, and a growth surface with a price on it is not a growth surface.

The receiver's screen is the whole design

  • A stranger lands on it having never heard of the site, so the ten-second rule applies at full force. It is not a new page. It is the topic phase of home with one line above it saying who sent it. Same topic type, same buttons, same clock after.
  • There is no Spin button on a dare. Spinning is what the dare exists to prevent. The way out is one quiet link under the buttons ("Or spin your own topic"), which is also the conversion path: it is how a stranger who came for one topic discovers the tool.
  • Both name states are drawn above, because the name is the open question below.

The link is stateless, which is the point

  • yapholic.com/?topic=<slug>&say=<word>&speak=<seconds>&from=<name>. No token, no row, no table, no expiry, nothing to clean up. The link describes itself and the existing deep link already reads the first parameter.
  • Every value needs a validator in front of it, per SPEC.md §8: the topic is a slug that exists or the parameter is dropped, speak is one of the lengths settings already offers or it is the default, say is one word capped at 24 characters, and none of it is ever rendered as anything but text.

What is unresolved, for the owner to call

  • The name in the URL. from= is text a stranger typed, shown on our page in our voice. Capped and escaped it is still a line somebody can put words into. Three ways: keep it (drawn in tab 2), drop it and say "Someone dared you" (tab 3), or take the name from the sender's account and only show it when they are signed in, which makes the nicest version of the link the one you get for having an account. Recommended: the third, with tab 3 as what a signed-out sender produces.
  • Which button leads on the receiver's done screen. "Spin again" is what turns them into a user; "Dare them back" is what spreads the link. Drawn with Spin again primary, to match the done screen the site already has. The other order is a real argument and this mock does not settle it.
  • Whether a dare can carry an owned genre's topic. A shared genre already has /g/<token> and a topic inside it has no global slug. Simplest first answer: dares carry bank topics only, and an owned genre keeps the share link it already has.
  • Whether the word is checked out loud. Drawn as a verdict card on done. It could also be silent, and merely part of the transcript. The card is the version that makes the dare worth filming.
