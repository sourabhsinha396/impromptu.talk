The receipt page: what it is, and what changes from v0

  • One column, and the first line is what they came for. "You have Pro." before any of the paperwork.
  • The plan, the money and the reference as rows, the same three-row shape the account page uses, so the two pages read as one product.
  • The primary button is a round, not the account page. Somebody who has just bought practice should be practising ten seconds later; the account is the quiet second button.
  • Pending is a real state and says so plainly. There are no webhooks, so a buyer who closes the tab before the redirect lands here; the link in their receipt settles it whenever they come back. v0 left this looking like a failure.
  • Failed says nothing was charged and gives the reference for the one case where something was.
  • The reference is monospaced, because the only reason it is on the page is that somebody may have to type or paste it into an email.
  • Subscription and one-time differ by one row (Renews) and one sentence (where cancelling lives). Nothing else on the page branches.
