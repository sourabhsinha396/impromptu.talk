Home and the round: what changes from v0, and why

  • Idle keeps v0's shape, because it was already the cleanest screen on the site. The genre chip and the settings gear sit above the question, the question wears the accent and states the speaking length from the setting ("a minute", "2 minutes"), and the one button says Spin. Nothing else on the screen.
  • Inside the round, the primary button says the duration. "Think for a minute" or "Think for 30 seconds", read off the setting, so nobody is surprised by the clock. "Speak now" stays the way to skip thinking.
  • The clock is one ring in both timed phases. Same size, same place, the word THINK or SPEAK under the digits, so the eye lands in the same spot when the phase changes. The ring turns warm in the last ten seconds.
  • The notes travel. Three stickies in prep with a live character count (80 cap); the same words as chips under the clock in speak, read-only. Placeholder says the rule: three words.
  • Done is three numbers. Day of streak with the flame, topics, minutes, as tiles; "Spin again" leads; "Same topic" beside it for the people who film retakes; one quiet line for strangers about where their streak lives.
  • Reset is a word under the buttons, not an arrow in the row. v0 led every row with a back arrow; here the row holds only what moves you forward (the primary button, then Pause or Speak now), and a quiet "Reset" underneath leaves and keeps nothing. From the topic it goes back to the start; from thinking or speaking it goes back to the topic with the notes intact, as v0's arrow did.
  • Spin is a button with the die on it, not a rotate icon. The rotate glyph read as "reload"; the die is the site's own mark and the word is the same one the first screen uses, so a new topic is always "Spin".
  • Chrome is untouched during thinking and speaking (header, footer hidden; the frame is a laptop filmed from across the room), and the spacing between the topic, the clock, the notes and the buttons is opened up so nothing crowds the ring.
  • Kept from v0: one question, one button, the topic in the accent and the button in ink, the flame pill as the only door to the streak, everything else behind the account button.
