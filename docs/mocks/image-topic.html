A picture instead of a sentence: proposal, not yet decided

This is not v0 parity - v0 never had picture prompts, so it needs its own case, logged in docs/DECISIONS.md once the owner picks a shape. This mock exists to have that conversation with something on screen, per the rulebook's "mock lands before the card." Nothing here is built.

What it is

  • A fourth kind of topic, alongside a sentence. Instead of "Meetings should be optional," the stage shows a picture; a person talks about what they see, tells the story they imagine, or answers whatever the caption asks. Genre and length still apply - it is a different topic, not a different round.
  • Text is optional on the picture, never required. Some rows are the bare picture ("describe what's happening"); others carry one short line under it, doing the job a caption does at the top of an interview-prep photo prompt ("what is this person about to regret?"). Both tabs above show the two rows.
  • Where it lives in the picker: not a fifth style (open/argue/teach/tell stays four, closed, per the rulebook) and not an eleventh genre. It reads as a topic shape, the same axis "coined style" already sits on for owned genres - so the natural home is a chip next to the genre chip on idle, a segmented control: Words / Pictures. Whichever is chosen, Spin draws from that pool for the genre in play. Open question for the owner: does every genre get a picture bank, or only a few (Culture, Everyday life, Relationships read naturally in pictures; Money & business or Philosophy less so)?
  • Prep and speak stay the ring. The clock is the fixed point in the round and must not move; the picture shrinks to a thumbnail above it once thinking starts, the way the topic sentence shrinks to topic-mid today, so the eye still lands on the same ring.

What is unresolved, for the owner to call

  • Source. A licensed stock set (cost per call or a flat library, attribution line under the image as drawn here), or AI-generated per topic (a per-round cost, and a style to keep consistent). Not a decision this mock makes.
  • The bank shape. Today Topic is one sentence per row. A picture topic needs an image reference (URL or storage key) and an optional caption in the same row shape, so this is a nullable column and a row kind, not a new table - in keeping with "fewer tables" in the rulebook. Filed as a question, not a schema change, until the owner picks a source.
  • The report. Everything downstream (the wave, the pace, the words leaned on) reads what was said, so it is unaffected either way - a picture topic still produces a transcript like any other.
