Administration: what this is, and what changes from v0

  • Two consoles, and this is the one with tools. /admin is Django's tables for the owner; /administration is four things that act on an account or on the browser in front of you. Everybody who is not a superuser gets a 404 here, signed out or signed in, and the routes behind it answer 404 too - a 403, a 401 or a 422 all confirm the path was guessed right.
  • The hub is the genres hub's cards, so a tool is a place you go rather than a link in a menu. Same card, same one-line blurb.
  • Staged topic never reaches the server. It writes one key in this browser, so two people filming off one login do not stage over each other and nothing about a rigged reel is written down beside real runs. The genre picks first, because the topic list is a thousand otherwise.
  • Giving Pro is one form, not eight fields in the admin. A reference invented on the spot and a session id for a checkout that never happened is how a gift silently does nothing at eleven at night. Two lengths, labelled by how long rather than by the plan's name.
  • Take it back expires the gift; it never says refunded. Nothing went back to anybody. A purchase that took money refuses outright, because that is a refund and a refund starts at the provider.
  • Payouts show the arithmetic the admin cannot. Who is owed what is derived from purchases and payouts, most owed first, with a missing PayPal address called out - that is a support mail waiting to happen. The amount is typed, since PayPal takes a fee off some transfers and the balance is a suggestion, not an instruction.
  • Outreach addresses the message and remembers who has had it. The rate in it is read off the same constant settlement pays. Nothing here sends anything.
