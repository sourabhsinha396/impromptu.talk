Affiliates: what this is, and what changes from v0

  • Every number on the page is read from the backend. The rate, the cookie window and the minimum payout come from the same constants settlement pays under, so no page can promise a number the code does not honour. v0 printed the rate in three places and the FAQ once got it wrong.
  • The pitch is three steps, not three paragraphs. Share the link, somebody buys within sixty days, you get 30% of what they paid. That is the whole product, and it fits on one screen above the questions.
  • The link is a field with a Copy button, the same control the account page and the share switch already use. Signed out, that block is one button to make an account, because the link cannot exist without one.
  • Three tiles, never two and one. People, sales, balance. Earned and paid sit under them as one sentence rather than as two more tiles, because they are how the balance was reached, not separate facts.
  • Nobody is named. An event is "New account" or "Lifetime bought", dated, with what it did to the balance. The people on that list did not sign up to be reported on.
  • A refund shows as its own line in red, taking the commission back, rather than the sale quietly vanishing from the list.
  • The sample faces say they are samples in a line under each, not in a footnote. v0 put three testimonials on the page with the disclaimer at the bottom, which is the wrong way round.
  • Payouts are a table under the address field, and the field says where money goes and from what balance. Nothing on this page sends money; the operator writes the row after PayPal has moved it.

Questions

How much is it, exactly?

30% of what the person was actually charged, in dollars. Prices differ by country, so a lifetime bought in India earns 30% of the rupee price, not of $39.

Do renewals earn?

No. A subscription earns on its first charge only. We are told about payments when somebody comes back to the site, and a renewal happens where we never hear it.

How long does a click last?

60 days. The last link clicked is the one that counts, and if they already had an account, whoever brought them in keeps them.

When do I get paid?

By PayPal, once your balance is over $10. Add your PayPal address on your referrals page and we send it by hand.

What if somebody refunds?

The commission comes off your balance. You will see the refund on your list as its own line.

Can I refer myself?

No. Your own purchases never earn, and neither does a second account of your own.
