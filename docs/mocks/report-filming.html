The report, sized for filming

Same round, same numbers, four drawings of it. The round: a minute on "Meetings should be optional." with a six second gap at 0:34, two seconds to start, 143 words a minute and four ums. Every number below is that one round, so the only thing changing between tabs is the drawing.

Why this exists. People film this. They hold a laptop up to a phone, or sit a phone on a stand pointed at the screen, and the reel is a vertical crop of the middle of that screen. Today's report is built for somebody reading a laptop at arm's length: a 40 pixel wave, 12.5 pixel numbers, four bands in two columns, sitting under the buttons at the bottom of the screen with nothing to spare - and the real done screen carries the read-back and the link to the full report under that, so those are already off the bottom. On a phone camera, at 40 percent scale, through one more round of compression, none of it is legible. Turn on What the phone sees and As filmed together on the Today tab: the crop takes about a third of the screen width, and the part of the report inside it is a grey smear.

What every filming drawing keeps

  • The same measures. The wave, the sentence, the longest gap, pace, time to start and ums a minute, each against its comfortable stretch. Nothing new is measured and nothing is dropped; only the drawing changes.
  • One screen, no scrolling. The frame here is the laptop screen and it clips: anything below the fold does not exist to somebody holding the laptop with both hands.
  • Nothing thinner than 4 pixels. A camera loses a hairline and a compressor eats what is left. The wave's strokes go from 1.5 to 4, the gap from 3 to 9, the band track from 6 to 14, the marker from 12 to 26.
  • Colour carries the verdict, not grey. The one measure outside its stretch is warm, in the value as well as on the track. Muted grey text is the first thing a camera loses, so it holds only what nobody needs to read.
  • The read-back stays a link. A paragraph of transcript is unreadable on camera at any size. It is there for the person, not the shot.

A · The column (the recommendation)

  • Everything in a 322 pixel column down the middle, inside the 352 pixel 9:16 crop of this screen with a margin either side. Turn on What the phone sees and nothing important is outside the dashes.
  • Order is what a viewer of the reel needs in that order: the topic, the minute, the one sentence, then the four measures, then what to do next time. The streak is a word in the top line, not three tiles: the header pill already carries it on every page.
  • Reads with no interaction, which matters when both hands are holding a laptop.
  • Costs: the wave is 322 pixels wide instead of 640, so a one second event is 5 pixels; and this is the busiest of the three, so it is the one that most needs the type sizes to hold.

B · One at a time

  • Three screens - your minute, the numbers, next time - at 56 to 60 pixel type, advancing every four seconds. The most legible drawing by a distance: at 40 percent scale it is still readable.
  • Costs: it takes twelve seconds to say what A says at once, a viewer who joins late sees a third of it, and a screen that moves on its own is a screen somebody has to wait for. Also the first thing on this site that animates without being asked to.

C · The full screen

  • The whole 900 pixels: the wave full width, the four measures in a row, the sentence at 38 pixels. Most information per second, and the wave is wide enough that the gap at 0:34 sits where you can point at it.
  • Costs: it does not survive a vertical crop. This is the one for somebody screen-recording or filming in landscape, not for a reel.

The setting

  • Named Report sized for filming, in the Additional settings page of the round's sheet (the tab above). The site already says "sized for a phone filming a laptop" about the topic, so this is the same sentence about the report.
  • Not "big fonts": it is not a zoom. The layout, the order, the stroke weights and what is dropped all change.
  • Off by default, and it changes only the report - the round itself is already sized for filming.
Pick one, or say which pieces of B and C should land in A. A is drawn as the one to build: it is the only drawing that fits the crop people actually post, and it needs nobody to press anything.
