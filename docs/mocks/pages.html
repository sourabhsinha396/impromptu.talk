impromptu.talk

About

impromptu.talk is a practice tool for speaking off the cuff. You press one button, a topic you did not pick lands in front of you, and you get a minute to think and a minute to talk. That is the whole product.

Why it exists

Most people are not bad at speaking. The fix is reps, and reps are hard to get alone, because the hard part is being handed a subject you would never have chosen.

So the topic is random on purpose. Choosing your own quietly removes the difficulty you came to practise.

How it works

  • A thousand topics across ten genres: everyday life, work and interviews, money, tech, science, health, philosophy and culture.
  • Four styles. The same topic can be an open answer, a hot take, an explanation or a story.
  • Prep and speaking lengths you can change, a minute each to start.

What it costs

Nothing, for the part you came for. There is no sign-up wall and no trial. An account is optional and carries your streak between devices.

One thing is paid: Pro keeps your whole history, writes your own genres and colours the page.

Who made it

A small independent project, built and run by one person. Bugs, topic suggestions and complaints land in the same inbox: hello@impromptu.talk.

Faster than email

The chat bubble in the corner of every page is the quickest way to ask something short.

What to include

  • For a refund: the address used at checkout and the date of the payment.
  • For a bug: what you pressed, and what happened instead.
  • For a topic that should not be in the bank: the words, so it can be found.

There is no contact form here on purpose. A form needs a mail service, spam handling and somewhere to keep what strangers type, and none of that would make a reply arrive sooner.

Refund Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026.

Almost all of impromptu.talk is free: the round, all thousand topics, the timers and a five day streak. The one thing you can buy is Pro. It is sold two ways, and which one you are buying is on the card you buy it from, on your receipt, and in your account afterwards.

Pro

  • Seven days, no questions. Ask within seven days of the payment and it is refunded in full.
  • A subscription renews until you cancel it. Monthly and yearly both do. Cancelling stops the next charge and keeps the period already paid for, so Pro runs out on the date it was always going to.
  • A 30 day pass and a lifetime never renew. There is nothing to cancel and nothing to forget about.
  • After seven days, ask anyway. Pro is a record of practice you did yourself, not a download; if it has not been worth it, say so.

When something is broken

If a paid feature does not do what its page said, that is a refund whenever you notice it, not within seven days.

How to ask

Write to hello@impromptu.talk with the address used at checkout and the date of the payment. Refunds are answered within three working days and the money goes back the way it came.
