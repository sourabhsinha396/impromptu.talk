Account pages: what changes from v0, and why

  • One narrow column. 440px, the headline in the display face, one sentence under it, the form, one line out. Nothing else on the page.
  • The fields look like the settings sheet's. Same border, same fill, same radius. A page and a sheet should not have two kinds of input.
  • The way out sits on the label row. "Forgotten?" beside Password on sign in, "8 or more characters" beside Password on sign up. Where the person is looking when they need it.
  • A refusal is one sentence above the form and the typing stays. Never a bare error code. The sentence has an ink edge; a notice ("a link is on its way") has an accent edge. The warm colour stays the clock's.
  • Name is optional. v0 refused a blank name. The rule is email and a password of eight characters and nothing else (SPEC §6). A name can be set later in settings.
  • One button, full width, ink. Our button at the row size. It never wears the accent.
  • Google and the captcha are drawn where they will land (cards 21 and 22). Neither is on the page until both of its keys are set. Google sits above the form on sign up and sign in only, never on the home page or in the menu.
  • Forgot and reset (card 20) share the frame. The forgot page answers the same sentence whether or not the address exists.
  • Short sentences everywhere. Simple words, one idea per line.
