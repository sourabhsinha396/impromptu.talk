Account pages: what changes from v0, and why

  • Every section is a card. v0 stacked a small grey heading, a form and a hint with nothing around them, so eight settings read as one long page of loose parts. A card gives each setting a heading, one sentence, its control and its own Save.
  • The page says who you are. The email and a Pro or Free tag sit under the headline. v0's /account never showed the address at all; it was on the other page.
  • Two pages, one tab bar. v0 ended /account with a section called "Additional settings" whose only content was a button to a page called "Additional settings". The bar names both pages and shows which one you are on.
  • The URL is /account/additional-settings. "additional" on its own reads as a fragment of a name.
  • Feedback lands in the card that did the work. "Saved" sits beside the button you pressed. v0 reloaded the page and put one banner at the top, so after saving your name you had to scroll up to learn it worked.
  • Save is off until something changes. A button that is always live and usually does nothing teaches you to ignore it.
  • Six colours you can see. v0 had a dropdown of colour names and a dot. Here the six are swatches, picking one previews it on the page at once, and only Save is Pro's.
  • Purchases show the amount. Name, date, what it charged, and the receipt. v0 ran them into one line of text.
  • The affiliate link is a field with a Copy button, not a line of text to select by hand.
  • Sign out everywhere asks first. It ends this session too, so a single click is the wrong shape.
  • Sharing is a switch. On or off, with the live link beside it, as SPEC §5 puts the off switch here rather than on the streak page. Off clears the token, so the link somebody was sent dies at once and sharing again makes a new one.
  • Order stays v0's on /account: subscription, purchases, affiliate link, colour. Subscription first is right, it is the only section whose answer changes on its own.
  • Short sentences, plain words. One idea per line.
  • Both pages are noindex, as v0 had them.
