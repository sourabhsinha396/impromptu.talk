Making and managing your own genres, from inside the picker

The picker is where somebody feels "these ten do not fit me", so it is where making and managing have to be readable. Five drawings of the same moment. D is the recommendation.

What is wrong today

  • The glyph lies. GenAI was saved with the square-pen icon, so the row's identity glyph reads as an edit button. Every other row uses that column for identity too, so one column now means two things and neither is trustworthy. Whatever else changes, an action never sits in the identity column: it goes to the right edge, where the check already lives.
  • Two shapes, one look. A genre row picks and closes; "Make your own genre" leaves the round and loads a page. They are drawn identically, so the second one is a trapdoor. A leaving row needs a visible tell, a chevron at the right edge.
  • Create is the only verb. Once you have a genre, "make your own" is the wrong door and there is no other. Adding a topic to GenAI means guessing that the create link is also the edit link. This is the actual complaint, and every variant below fixes it.
  • No handle on the group. "Yours" is a label with nothing to press. It is the natural home for the one door that covers all of them.

The five

  • Today. What ships now, for comparison. Note how the genre's own icon is the thing that looks pressable.
  • A: a pencil on the row. Body picks, pencil edits, per genre. The clearest mapping there is, and it teaches which genres are yours because built-ins never carry one. Costs a second target inside a row; it wants 34px and it stays visible on touch, where hover does not exist.
  • B: manage on the group's heading. Rows stay pure selection, one door for all of them, nothing new to hit inside a row. Cheapest and safest. Weaker at teaching that a particular genre can be edited, and "manage" is a word about the system rather than about talking.
  • C: one row, and the label changes with the state. The suggestion, held to the copy rule. "Make or manage genre" is two verbs bolted together and it reads as admin; the same row can just say what is true: Make your own genre when you have none, Add or edit your genres once you do. One target, no new chrome. It still cannot point at one genre.
  • D: the pencil and the heading, together. The heading carries "Edit" for the group, each row carries a pencil for itself, and the bottom row goes back to being honestly about creating: "Make a new genre" with a plus. Three affordances, but they are the three questions a person actually has, and each is answered where it is asked. This is the one to build.
  • E: the first time. With nothing of your own there is nothing to manage, so every variant collapses to the same single row plus one line saying what it does. The line is worth its ink once, and only here: it is the only place the feature has to explain itself.

Small calls that ride along

  • Check and pencil share the right edge. On the selected row the check sits left of the pencil rather than the pencil moving; the check's column is the one that must not jump as you scroll.
  • The count is a fact, not a warning. "3 of 10" sits by the heading, muted. It says the cap once, here, instead of refusing later on the tenth.
  • The pencil says whose it is. Its label is "Edit GenAI", not "Edit", so a screen reader is not read three identical buttons.
  • Leaving mid-round. Every one of these doors abandons the sheet for a page. The editor's link carries the current genre back, so Done returns you to the picker with your pick intact rather than to a cold home.
  • Without Pro nothing here is hidden. The group still sits last, the pencil is still drawn on the genres you already made, and the page behind it is where the paywall speaks. A greyed pencil in a list is a worse teacher than a page that says what Pro does.
