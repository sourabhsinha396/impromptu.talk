Streak page: what changes from v0, and why

  • The three tiles are the done screen's tiles. v0 drew a second, flatter stats row here; the page you land on from the flame now looks like the screen that sent you, and one component serves both.
  • Free gets a five-day strip, not a five-square heatmap. The rule is five days (owner's call, card 16), so the calendar is five named days you can read from a sofa: today ringed, spoken days filled. The year heatmap is Pro's: a week per column, time left to right, month names over the columns, a dashed square for a day the freeze held. Every window is the plan's number, never the template's.
  • Recent shows the genre beside the time. A list of sentences with "2d ago" told you when; the genre glyph tells you what you have been practising, which is what a person scanning the list wants to know. On a phone the genre drops and the time stays.
  • The pitch is one sentence and only when true. "Pro would have held it at N" appears only when the Pro rule really would, and payments are on. Nothing on the page is taken away to sell it; the streak itself stays free.
  • Share sits after the button, not before. The primary action is back into the tool. Share appears only signed in and with runs: the link and "Open it", nothing else. Turning it off lives in Additional settings.
  • One column, left-aligned, 960px, the genre page's frame, so the two content pages read alike. Phone: the tiles wrap, the strip stays five across, the year grid scrolls to today.
