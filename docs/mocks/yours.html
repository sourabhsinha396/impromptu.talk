Your genres: what this is, and what changes from v0

  • They are genres, not packs. One table holds the bank and the ones people write, so the picker has one row shape and the editor has one vocabulary. Routes are /genres/yours and /genres/yours/[slug], under the genres hub that already exists; a shared one is /g/[token].
  • The list is the genres hub's cards. Same card, same count line, plus a pencil. "Make one" is the dashed card in the same grid rather than a button somewhere else, so the thing you make appears where you were already looking. The header line says 3 of 10, which is the cap said once instead of a refusal later.
  • One paste box, not three. "Build, import, CSV" were three names for typing lines into a field. Every line is a topic; a line ending in a style is tagged with it, and a comma in an ordinary sentence stays in the sentence. New in v1: the select underneath says what an untagged line becomes, so forty interview questions in one style cost one pick instead of forty tails.
  • A topic is edited in place, not in a dialog: the row becomes a field and a style box, and the style box takes one of ours or words of your own ("IELTS part 2"). v0 opened a modal with a select and a second hidden field for a custom name.
  • Sharing is a switch with the live link beside it, the same control the streak page uses. Off means the link somebody already has stops working, and it says so.
  • Without Pro the page is not hidden. The genres you made stay listed and practisable and their links keep working; making, pasting and editing say what they need. Deleting stays open, because nobody should be locked in with ten genres they cannot tidy.
  • The shared page is for a stranger. Icon, name, "Ada's genre", every topic with its style, and one button that starts a round in it. No account, nothing copied, noindex.
